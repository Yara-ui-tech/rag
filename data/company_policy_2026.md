# Nexus Horizon Global Employee Policy & Operational Guidelines (2026 Edition)
Author: People & Culture Department
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

## 1. Remote Work & Home Office Equipment
All full-time engineers and staff are entitled to a one-time home office setup reimbursement of $1,850.
Eligible equipment includes:
- Ergonomic chairs (certified BIFMA standard)
- Motorized standing desks
- 4K monitors (up to 2 units)
- Noise-canceling headsets with boom microphone

Reimbursement claims must be submitted via Portal NexusExpense within 45 calendar days of purchase, accompanied by original itemized receipts.

## 2. Emergency Deployment & On-Call Allowances
Engineers participating in the Primary Incident Response Roster receive the following compensations:
- Weekday on-call standby rate: $75 per 12-hour shift.
- Weekend on-call standby rate: $150 per 12-hour shift.
- Active incident resolution premium: 1.5x regular hourly wage for each hour logged during an active Sev-1 or Sev-2 outage.
- Emergency deployment meal allowance: $65 per person for incidents extending beyond 4 consecutive hours. Alcohol is strictly non-reimbursable.

## 3. Learning and Conference Budget
Every team member has an annual personal professional development allocation of $3,200.
This budget can be applied towards:
- Certified AWS, GCP, or Kubernetes certifications.
- AI, Machine Learning, and Large Language Model training bootcamps.
- International technical conferences (travel must be booked at least 21 days in advance through Concur).

## 4. Annual Leave & Wellness Days
- Standard Annual Vacation: 25 days paid time off per calendar year.
- "Recharge Fridays": The last Friday of every quarter is a company-wide paid mental health day with no internal meetings or Slack expectations.
- Sick Leave: 12 days per year, no doctor's note needed for absences under 3 consecutive days.
